ABOUT ME

Welcome.

I am Siyang Wu, a Research Assistant in the Data Science and Analytics (DSA) Thrust at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), HKUST(GZ).

My research focuses on quantum error correction (QEC) and decoding.

During my master's studies, I worked on applications of quantum machine learning (QML) to high-energy physics data analysis.

I earned my bachelor's degree in Physics from Taishan College, Shandong University, in 2023, and my master's degree in Physics from the Institute of Frontier and Interdisciplinary Science, Shandong University, in 2026.
